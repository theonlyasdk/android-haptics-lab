Haptics Lab is a fully offline, open-source vibration laboratory for Android.

• Probe hardware: vibrator presence, amplitude control, composition primitives
• Generate patterns: discrete pulses, sine, square, sawtooth, triangle, chirp sweeps, LFO modulation
• Visualize waveforms on an interactive 2D graph with playhead, zoom and pan
• Benchmark impulses: 18 one-shot clicks/taps/thuds plus 17 looping rhythms (Morse, engine idle, siren, breathing)
• Play once or loop any pattern, with live timing and amplitude preview
• No ads, no tracking, no network access — VIBRATE permission only
